## Research Question

Are recoverable states reachable in selected cases under the simplified 2D dynamics when control inputs are optimized directly rather than generated by the hand-designed controller?

---

## Why was this question important?

Phase31 left an ambiguity: maybe the benchmark was physically unreachable under the simplified dynamics, or maybe the tested controller architectures were not finding the right trajectory structure. Phase32 tried to separate feasibility from controller design.

---

## Previous evidence

The audit cites `analysis/phase31_global_transfer_solver/summary.md` as showing crossings without recoverability. It cites `analysis/phase32_direct_optimal_control/summary.md` for Phase32 and notes that CasADi was unavailable, so the experiment used a SciPy direct-shooting fallback.

---

## Competing hypotheses

- Recoverable states are reachable, but the explicit transfer families miss them.
- Recoverable states are not reachable for the selected cases under the simplified dynamics and constraints.
- Objective choice determines whether direct shooting finds crossings or recoverability.
- The solver can find useful probes but not enough evidence for a general optimal-control claim.
- The result may be a numerical artifact of a small selected sample.

---

## Why was this experiment designed this way?

The audit records a direct-shooting probe with horizon `512` physics steps and `64` control intervals, using `4` solves per objective. This design optimized controls over a finite horizon to look for recoverable states in selected cases. It was a probe, not a production controller and not a proof of optimality.

---

## What result was expected?

If Phase31 failed because of controller architecture, direct shooting might find lower recoverability distance or selected recoverable states. If the selected cases were genuinely infeasible under the simplified dynamics, direct shooting would also fail.

---

## What actually happened?

The audit records Phase32 as a SciPy direct-shooting fallback because CasADi was unavailable. It also flags overclaim risk in phrases like "optimal control outperform heuristic architectures" and safer wording: direct-shooting probes reached lower recoverability distance in selected cases. The audit states that recoverability was reachable under the simplified 2D dynamics in selected solves, not as a broad theorem.

---

## Interpretation

Phase32 supports the narrow claim that selected recoverable states could be reached by direct-shooting probes under simplified dynamics. It does not prove global optimality, does not prove benchmark-wide feasibility, and does not establish an online controller.

---

## Alternative explanations

- The selected cases may have been easier than the unresolved benchmark cases.
- The objective may have encouraged recoverability distance without producing robust closed-loop behavior.
- The direct-shooting solution may be sensitive to initialization or solver settings.
- A different direct-transcription or collocation method might produce different results.

---

## Reviewer criticism

Reviewer #2 would object to calling this "optimal control" too strongly because the audit says CasADi/IPOPT was unavailable and SciPy direct shooting was used. They would also ask how many cases were tested and whether the selected solves generalize.

---

## Sean's response

The honest answer is: Phase32 was a diagnostic optimization probe. It showed that recoverability-like states were reachable in selected simplified-dynamics solves, but repository evidence is insufficient to claim global optimality, general feasibility, or a solved online control method.

---

## If you repeated this experiment

I would run a manifest-backed case set, compare direct shooting with direct collocation if tooling is available, report solver convergence and sensitivity, and keep the claim limited to reachable states under the simplified model.

---

## Future direction

The next logical step was Phase33: analyze the optimized trajectories to extract controller-relevant structure rather than treating direct shooting as the final controller.
