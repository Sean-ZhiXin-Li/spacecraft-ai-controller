## Recommended Review Schedule

Daily 45-minute minimum:

- 10 min: exact benchmark numbers.
- 10 min: crossing vs recoverability explanation.
- 10 min: radial/tangential velocity and circular velocity.
- 10 min: Phase34 and Phase37B evidence.
- 5 min: one safe claim and one dangerous overclaim.

Two-hour deep review:

- 30 min: physics cards.
- 30 min: recoverability cards.
- 30 min: Phase31-37 experiment cards.
- 20 min: learning failure cards.
- 10 min: reproducibility cards.
