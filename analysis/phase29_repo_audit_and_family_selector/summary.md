## Research Answers

1. Does Burn-A family selection reduce dead windows? `no`. Best dead-window count: `12` from `phase29_baseline_phase22_burn_a`.
2. Does it increase crossing-producing trajectories? `no`. Best crossing count: `12` from `phase29_angular_momentum_targeted_burn_a`.
3. Does it improve crossing sync error? `no`.
4. Does it produce near-recoverable or recoverable crossing? Near recoverable best `5`; recoverable best `0`.
5. Was the Phase 28 wrong-trajectory-family hypothesis supported? `not by this selector run`.
6. What should Phase 30 test? A wider Burn-A search over transfer-family endpoints with explicit constraints on Burn-A-end orbit crossing, not another Burn-B correction.

## Success Criteria

- Minimum, full repo audit completed and Burn-A-end geometry instrumented: `met`.
- Moderate, dead windows reduced: `not met`.
- Strong, crossing-producing family increased: `not met`.
- Major, recoverable/CAPTURE/success improve: `not met`.

## Honest Interpretation

- Best mean quality variant: `phase29_angular_momentum_targeted_burn_a`.
- This phase tests a hypothesis generated by Phase 28; it does not prove causality from historical correlation alone.
- If performance does not move, the next bottleneck is likely that the selector needs a broader Burn-A endpoint search, not that late insertion should be tuned again.
